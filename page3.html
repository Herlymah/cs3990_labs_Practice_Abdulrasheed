Dream Careers

  1. GIS Analyst

    A Geographic Information Systems (GIS) Analyst uses spatial data to solve complex geographic problems. This role combines technology, geography, and data analysis to create meaningful insights for various industries.

    My journey to becoming a GIS Analyst is a strategic progression built on my existing surveying degree and current computer systems technology diploma. The surveying background provides a strong foundation in spatial measurements, topography, and geographical data collection, which directly translates to GIS principles. By pursuing a computer systems technology diploma, I'm developing critical technical skills in data management, programming, and spatial analysis software. This combination of surveying expertise and emerging technological skills creates a unique pathway to my dream career as a GIS Analyst.

Favorite Movies

  1. Titanic

    A sweeping romantic epic that intertwines a fictional love story with the historical tragedy of the RMS Titanic's maiden voyage.

    Favorite Scene: The iconic "I'm flying" moment at the bow of the ship, where Jack (Leonardo DiCaprio) stands behind Rose (Kate Winslet), spreading her arms wide as they experience a moment of pure freedom and connection. This scene symbolizes their breaking free from societal constraints and experiencing unbridled joy, just moments before the impending tragedy.

    Watch/Order: Amazon Prime Video

  2. The Apocalypse

    A gripping disaster film that explores humanity's resilience in the face of global catastrophe.

    Favorite Scene: The moment when the surviving group discovers a hidden underground sanctuary, representing hope and human ingenuity amidst complete societal breakdown. This scene powerfully illustrates the human capacity for survival and community-building even in the most desperate circumstances.

    Watch/Order: Netflix

Favorite Books

  1. Americanah

    A profound exploration of race, identity, and migration through the eyes of Ifemelu, a Nigerian woman navigating life between Africa and America.

    Favorite Page/Passage: The section where Ifemelu writes a provocative blog post about race in America, dissecting the nuanced experiences of being a non-American Black person in the United States. This passage brilliantly deconstructs racial identity, revealing the complex layers of cultural understanding and personal experience that define immigrant perspectives.

    Buy/Download: Goodreads

  2. The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck

    A counterintuitive self-help guide that challenges traditional positive thinking paradigms.

    Favorite Page/Passage: The chapter discussing the importance of choosing what to care about, where Manson argues that constant positivity is exhausting and that true happiness comes from accepting limitations and focusing on what truly matters. The passage suggests that by being selective about our priorities, we can live more authentic and meaningful lives.

    Buy/Download: Goodreads
